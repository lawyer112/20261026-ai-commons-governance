# 人工智能何以共产——社会知识、贡献回报与共同治理

An AI Commons: Social Knowledge, Contributor Compensation, and Shared Governance

AI 依靠社会长期积累的知识，却通常由企业提供收费服务，贡献者与公众应如何获得回报和参与？本文把这一分配直觉展开为可讨论的权利、预算与组织问题。

## 论文简介

通过概念分析、规范论证与制度比较，文章区分知识、受保护表达、模型资产和运行服务，并以明确变量推导免费基本服务的必要预算条件。没有组织试点或效果估计。

- 社会知识来源本身不足以推出全部模型共同所有或所有服务永久免费；公共回报需要互惠、基本能力与问责等额外规范理由。
- 可识别贡献的直接补偿与面向公众的基本服务、收益分享和治理参与，应分别落实，不能互相抵销。
- 公益持有主体、贡献者组织与运营公司可以通过可执行权利协作；是否采用该安排，须与税收、采购、合作社和直接公共供给比较。

预算式仅给出单期、固定服务质量下的必要条件，不是商业预测。组织方案尚待法域适配、合同审查与实际试点；文献为选择性检索，核验深度不一。

两份 PDF 均含完整正文、方法、限制与参考文献。计算复核与 AI 辅助自查的范围在正文说明，不等同于外部学术审稿。

## English

### About the paper

AI draws on accumulated social knowledge while firms commonly charge for its services. How should contributors and the public obtain returns and participation? The paper develops this distributional question through rights, budgets, and institutional arrangements.

**[Complete English PDF](en.pdf) · [Chinese PDF](zh.pdf) · [Discussion and feedback](https://github.com/lawyer112/20261026-ai-commons-governance/issues)**

Author: lawyer112 · Paper ID: 20261026-ai-commons-governance · Content version: 0.1.1 · Edition date: 28 October 2026

Conceptual analysis, normative argument, and institutional comparison distinguish knowledge, protected expression, model assets, and operational services. Explicit variables yield a necessary budget condition for free basic services. No organizational pilot or effect estimate is reported.

- Social origins alone do not establish collective ownership of every model or permanent free provision. Public returns require additional premises such as reciprocity, basic capabilities, and accountability.
- Direct compensation for identifiable contributions and public returns through basic access, benefit sharing, and governance should be secured separately rather than offset against one another.
- A public interest asset holder, contributor organizations, and an operating firm could collaborate through enforceable rights. Adoption requires comparison with taxation, procurement, cooperatives, and direct public provision.

The budget gives a necessary condition for one period at fixed service quality, not a business forecast. Jurisdictional adaptation, contract review, and actual pilots remain outstanding. Literature selection is targeted and verification depth varies.

Both PDFs contain the complete text, methods, limitations, and references. The paper explains the scope of computational verification and AI-assisted self-checks; these are not external academic review.
